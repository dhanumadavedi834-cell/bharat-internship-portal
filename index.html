Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible to apply for internships on InternIndia?

Indian students pursuing Diploma, Undergraduate, or Postgraduate degrees from recognized universities are eligible. Specific eligibility criteria vary by listing.

How is the monthly stipend distributed for government internships?

Stipends for PM Internship and PSU roles are directly credited into students' Aadhaar-linked bank accounts via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
